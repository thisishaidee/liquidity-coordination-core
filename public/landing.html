The coordination layer

Liquidity should be ready when the work is.

As agents execute more tasks, their funding needs become harder to manage one wallet and one request at a time. NERITH gives businesses a coordinated view of upcoming obligations, available capital and the actions needed to close a gap.

01 / ANTICIPATE

See the need before the block.

Model upcoming obligations against fresh, authorized balances and identify tasks that are covered, may need conversion, or remain blocked.

02 / COORDINATE

Allocate with explicit permission.

Respect agent-level boundaries while allowing designated agents to draw on approved business wallets. Reservations help prevent double allocation.

03 / VERIFY

Know what actually settled.

Evaluate conversion routes, prepare unsigned transactions for a business-controlled signer, and verify on-chain settlement before balances are trusted again.

Control stays with the business

Coordination without custody.

NERITH does not take possession of business funds or agent private keys. It works within the permissions and wallet boundaries your business defines.

Scoped agent credentials

Each agent operates through its own API identity and authorized access.

Business-controlled signing

Transactions are prepared unsigned. Your designated signer retains control.

Policy-aware allocation

Business wallet access is explicit, never assumed across agents.

Settlement is checked

A quote or reservation is not treated as a completed transfer.
