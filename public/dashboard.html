Access model
Telegram-linked business access

This dashboard requires an active Telegram-linked business session. Owners and admins can manage workspace data; analysts have read-only access. Sessions expire after 12 hours, and disabling a member or changing their dashboard role invalidates their existing session.

Manage invitations in Business Setup ↗
Access boundaries

Dashboard roles are separate from Telegram request permissions. Only the workspace owner can invite another dashboard admin. Notification preferences and a full audit-history screen are not active here.
